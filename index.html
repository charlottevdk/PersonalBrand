How I work

My core quadrants

Everyone has qualities — and every quality has a pitfall when overdone. These core quadrants (Ofman) map my strengths, what happens when I push them too far, and what I am working on.

Drive & independence
Strength
I take ownership and finish what I start — the Copilot initiative I set up unassigned, my 8-week training programme.
Pitfall
Wanting to do everything myself; impatience with other people's pace; holding too much control.
Challenge
Delegate, give trust, leave room for others' ways of working.
Allergy
Passivity and waiting around in others.
Analytical thinking
Strength
From data to insight to recommendation — the MIS Foot intelligence report, the Jetpak root-cause analysis.
Pitfall
Over-analysing; preferring a ‘perfect’ analysis over a timely decision.
Challenge
Cut knots in time; act pragmatically on 80% of the information.
Allergy
Superficiality and gut-feel decisions.
Directness
Strength
I name what I see, without detours.
Pitfall
Correcting too sharply; attacking the other person's premise head-on.
Challenge
Frame my position diplomatically; let the other person finish first.
Allergy
Vagueness and beating about the bush.
